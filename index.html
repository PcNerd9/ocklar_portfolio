Music Promotion & Public Relations Brand

OCKLAR Promotions Limited is a Nigerian-based music promotion and public relations company founded in 2021. The company focuses on amplifying artists, building strong creative identities, and executing high-impact publicity campaigns across digital and live platforms.

Founded by Ayomide Adewunmi (professionally known as OcklarPR), Ocklar Promotions Limited specializes in artist promotion, brand storytelling, event publicity, and audience growth through strategic communication and cultural relevance.

From managing artist visibility and brand narratives to organizing live events and digital campaigns, Ocklar Promotions Limited exists to elevate creative voices while fostering genuine connections with fans and industry stakeholders.
